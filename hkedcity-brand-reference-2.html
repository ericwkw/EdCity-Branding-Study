Brand foundation

Mission

We are the super-connector and super-enabler for Hong Kong education.

Values

Curious · Courageous · Passionate · Grateful

Logo meaning

Blue represents the boundless sky; green represents nature’s vitality. The young eye expresses curiosity, creativity, and dreams—inviting exploration of the world, knowledge, and the future.

Three design commitments

We Make Things Clear

Zhou Youguang 周有光 · Shared standards, clarity, inclusive access.

Clear language, consistent patterns, and quiet intelligence help people find, understand, and act on what matters.

We Create Structure

Otl Aicher · Systems, hierarchy, legibility, clear pathways.

Modular layouts, repeatable components, clear labels, and meaningful status cues make complexity navigable.

We Assist

Naoto Fukasawa 深澤直人 · Familiar interaction, intuitive usefulness, human agency.

Support should feel natural, relevant, and recognisable. Clear choices keep people in control.

Functional attributes

The non-negotiable baseline across every touchpoint. Usability is not a stylistic preference.

Clear

Use plain language, clear hierarchy, familiar patterns, and visible next steps.

Credible

Show relevant sources, recency, ownership, permissions, and limitations. Be confident without overstating certainty.

Enabling

Provide relevant guidance and useful tools while preserving choice and control.

Inclusive

Support accessibility and recognise different roles, abilities, confidence levels, and contexts.

Calm

Use measured language, manageable steps, and clear status. Avoid alarmism, gamified pressure, and performance theatre.

Expressive attributes

Bring these forward on brand-rich surfaces without compromising the baseline.

Curious

Invite exploration, discovery, connections, and lifelong learning.

Optimistic

Make possibilities and progress visible without becoming overly idealistic or promotional.

Human

Reflect diverse educational lives, goals, constraints, and agency.

Vibrant

Use colour, imagery, illustration, and motion purposefully without weakening clarity or calm.

Connected

Connect learners, families, teachers, schools, resources, and opportunities.

Function comes first. Vibrant must still be clear. Clever must still be familiar. Optimistic must still be credible.

Personality boundaries

Be

Confident, factual, and calm.
Helpful and empowering.
Clever without showing off.
Relaxed and focused on progress.
Inclusive and context-aware.

Avoid

Bureaucratic or unfriendly language.
Controlling, critical, or bossy behaviour.
“Magic AI” buzzwords and exaggerated claims.
Alarmism, manufactured urgency, or pressure.
One-size-fits-all flows.

Decision check
Do layers and gradients invite discovery while controls stay clear?

Possibilities may be open-ended; navigation and choices must not be. Use gradients for change and transition—not exaggerated AI claims.

Decision gate

Review a specific screen, flow, or concept. Check only what you can demonstrate—not what you intend.

Resolve baseline gaps before adding expression.

Unchecked means unresolved—not automatically failed. All checked means ready for testing, not proof of usability.
