Our logo’s meaning

Blue represents the boundless sky; green represents nature’s vitality. The young eye expresses curiosity, creativity, and dreams—inviting exploration of the world, knowledge, and the future.

Three design commitments

Make things clear, create structure, and provide useful support without taking control.

01 / Clarity

We make things clear.

Use clear language, consistent patterns, and quiet intelligence to help people find, understand, and act on what matters.

Reference: Zhou Youguang 周有光
Shared standards, clarity, inclusive access.
02 / Form

We create structure.

Use modular layouts, repeatable components, clear labels, and meaningful status cues so complexity becomes navigable.

Reference: Otl Aicher
Systems, hierarchy, legibility, clear pathways.
03 / Behaviour

We assist.

Make support natural rather than intrusive, using familiar patterns, relevant context, and choices that keep people in control.

Reference: Naoto Fukasawa 深澤直人
Familiar interaction, intuitive usefulness, human agency.
The non-negotiable baseline

Functional attributes

These apply across every journey and touchpoint. Usability is a requirement, not a stylistic preference.

Clear

Makes complex education information easy to understand and act on.

In practiceUse plain language, clear hierarchy, familiar patterns, and visible next steps.

Credible

Builds trust through accuracy, transparency, and evidence.

In practiceMake sources, data recency, ownership, permissions, and limitations visible.

Enabling

Helps people move from information to meaningful action.

In practiceProvide relevant guidance, useful tools, and contextual recommendations while preserving choice and control.

Inclusive

Recognises different needs, abilities, confidence levels, and contexts.

In practiceAdapt content and guidance, support accessibility, and avoid one-size-fits-all assumptions.

Calm

Creates progress without unnecessary pressure.

In practiceUse measured language, manageable next steps, and clear status cues. Avoid alarmism and gamified pressure.

How the brand comes to life

Expressive attributes

Express these more fully on brand-rich surfaces, without compromising the functional baseline.

Curious

Encourages exploration, discovery, and lifelong learning.

In practiceUse prompts, stories, and learning pathways that invite new connections and possibilities.

Optimistic

Feels forward-looking, capable, and constructive.

In practiceFrame challenges as navigable and make progress visible without becoming overly promotional.

Human

Feels approachable, respectful, and grounded in real educational lives.

In practiceShow diverse people and everyday learning contexts. Recognise goals, constraints, and agency.

Vibrant

Brings energy, warmth, and memorability.

In practiceUse colour, imagery, illustration, and motion purposefully without compromising clarity or calm.

Connected

Makes education feel like a shared ecosystem.

In practiceConnect learners, families, teachers, schools, resources, and opportunities visually and verbally.

Function comes first.

A vibrant experience must still be clear. A clever interaction must still be familiar. An optimistic message must still be credible.

Personality boundaries

Confident and helpful. Approachable and measured. Always aware of the context.

BeAvoid
Confident, factual, and calmExaggerated certainty, alarmism, or pressure
Helpful and empoweringControlling, critical, or bossy
Clever without showing off“Magic AI” buzzwords or exaggerated AI claims
Relaxed and focused on progressPushy language or manufactured urgency
Inclusive and context-awareOne-size-fits-all flows
Approachable and respectfulBureaucratic, overly business-like, or unfriendly language
